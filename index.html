Who I am

Designer & Developer

Born in St. Lucia, I immigrated to the United States with my family at the age of four and was raised in Houston, Texas, where I graduated high school in 2015. Growing up in a vibrant Caribbean-American community sparked my creativity and early passion for technology, beginning with designing visually aesthetic, responsive websites for my community.

Recently married, I’m now a junior pursuing a Bachelor’s in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics. My journey has evolved from web development to machine learning, computer vision, and robotics engineering, where I’ve gained hands-on experience with real-time image detection models, deep learning architectures, and AI-powered applications showcased on my GitHub.

With a strong work ethic and passion for innovation, I’m excited to continue building my career in AI and robotics—bringing both technical expertise and creative problem-solving to a forward-thinking company.
